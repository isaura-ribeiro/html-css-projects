Mysterious Park Bench Vanishes Overnight

Residents wake up to find the bench missing.

By Jane Doe

Residents of Maple Square were surprised Tuesday morning when one of the park's oldest benches disappeared overnight. The bench, a favorite meeting spot for locals, vanished without any visible signs of damage or removal.

City officials confirmed that no maintenance work had been scheduled, and security footage is being reviewed. While authorities continue investigating, the mysterious disappearance has sparked jokes and theories across social media, leaving residents eager for answers.
